Comparative Analysis of Modern TCP Congestion Control

Spring 2026

Open project website

Evaluated CUBIC, Reno, BBRv3, and Vegas across controlled tc/netem experiments, including terrestrial, LEO, GEO, delay-only, and packet-loss conditions. The final analysis covers 460 iperf3 trials across 23 link conditions and shows BBRv3 leading throughput and utilization in high-loss, high-latency regimes, with a clear retransmission tradeoff.

Drone Navigation Through Constrained Gate Environments

Fall 2025

Open project site

Built an autonomous drone pipeline that uses an FPV camera and ArUco marker detection to localize constrained gates, then plans and executes trajectories with a discrete-time receding-horizon LQR controller.

1 of 2

The system was validated in MuJoCo with propeller-thrust actuation and then adapted for DJI Tello hardware using SDK-level position and yaw commands.

Solitaire Rules
  1. Move all 52 cards to the four foundation piles to win.
  2. Foundations build upward by suit from Ace to King.
  3. Tableau columns build downward by rank and must alternate red and black cards.
  4. Only face-up cards can move.
  5. A face-up tableau stack can move only when every card in the stack is already in descending alternating order.
  6. Empty tableau columns can only receive a King or a valid stack that starts with a King.
  7. Click the stock to draw one card to the waste pile.
  8. Only the top waste card can move to a tableau column or foundation.
  9. The top card of a foundation can move back to the tableau if it fits.
  10. When the stock is empty, click it to turn the waste pile back into the stock.
  11. When a face-down tableau card is uncovered, it turns face up.

Meditation with Clancy

Via mindful.org

  1. Take a seat Find place to sit that feels calm and quiet to you.
  2. Set a time limit If you’re just beginning, it can help to choose a short time, such as five or 10 minutes.
  3. Notice your body You can sit in a chair with your feet on the floor, you can sit loosely cross-legged, you can kneel—all are fine. Just make sure you are stable and in a position you can stay in for a while.
  4. Feel your breath Follow the sensation of your breath as it goes in and as it goes out.
  5. Notice when your mind has wandered Inevitably, your attention will leave the breath and wander to other places. When you get around to noticing that your mind has wandered—in a few seconds, a minute, five minutes—simply return your attention to the breath.
  6. Be kind to your wandering mind Don’t judge yourself or obsess over the thoughts’ content you find yourself lost in. Just come back.
  7. Close with kindness When you’re ready, gently lift your gaze (if your eyes are closed, open them). Take a moment and notice any sounds in the environment. Notice how your body feels right now. Notice your thoughts and emotions.
