Solitaire
Solitaire Rules
  1. Move all 52 cards to the four foundation piles to win.
  2. Foundations build upward by suit from Ace to King.
  3. Tableau columns build downward by rank and must alternate red and black cards.
  4. Only face-up cards can move.
  5. A face-up tableau stack can move only when every card in the stack is already in descending alternating order.
  6. Empty tableau columns can only receive a King or a valid stack that starts with a King.
  7. Click the stock to draw one card to the waste pile.
  8. Only the top waste card can move to a tableau column or foundation.
  9. The top card of a foundation can move back to the tableau if it fits.
  10. When the stock is empty, click it to turn the waste pile back into the stock.
  11. When a face-down tableau card is uncovered, it turns face up.
